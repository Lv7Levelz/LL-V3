FAQ

Questions before you level up?

Here are the most common questions local businesses ask before starting a website project.

Do you host the website?

We can use low-cost or free static hosting where appropriate, or work with your existing domain and hosting setup.

Can I keep my current domain?

Yes. Your existing domain can usually be connected to the new website without changing your business address online.

Can my old website be restored?

Yes, provided the existing site is backed up before migration. We recommend keeping a complete copy before any live-domain change.

Do you provide SEO?

We build local SEO foundations into the site and can also support deeper local SEO work depending on the project.

Can customers contact me through WhatsApp?

Yes. WhatsApp click-to-chat can be built into the website alongside email, phone and enquiry forms.

How long does a website take?

Timing depends on scope and content, but smaller local-business websites can move quickly once information and approvals are available.
